## Answer policy
- Ground in brick.md. IH ≠ law.  
- Headline mechanism = **5-year temporary moratorium** preventing state/local enforcement of laws regulating AI models/systems/ADS in interstate commerce, with facilitation / generally-applicable / criminal carve-outs (SEC. 6).  
- Also: National AI Action Plan to Congress (SEC. 5).  
- Normalized action: `limits_state_regulation` (center of this brick).
